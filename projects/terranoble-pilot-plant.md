# Terranoble and Pilot Plant

## Project overview

The AutoCAD model brings together the facility layout, equipment placement, and utility routing for Terranoble and its pilot plant. The portfolio includes an interactive 3D model and annotated screenshots covering the retort bank, processing and handling equipment, and cooling-water recirculation.

## Utility color legend

| Color | Service |
| --- | --- |
| Dark blue | Cooling-water supply |
| Medium blue | Treated cooling-water return for recirculation |
| Cyan | Process water |
| Yellow | Compressed air |
| Bright pink | Steam |
| Light pink | Drainage |
| Brown | Safety-valve piping |
| Pale yellow | Vent piping |

- [Filler utility connections](../assets/terranoble/filler-utilities.png). Cyan process water, yellow compressed air, and bright pink steam run alongside the conveyor. The close-up highlights the drops and valve assemblies at the filler connection area.
- [Feed and transfer equipment](../assets/terranoble/feed-area.png). The view shows inclined conveyors, hoppers, and adjacent process equipment. Cyan process-water and bright pink steam branches run through the equipment area, a dark blue cooling-water main crosses overhead, and a light pink drain run is visible near floor level.
- [Cooling tower and water recirculation](../assets/terranoble/cooling-water-loop.png). Dark blue marks the cooling-water supply, and medium blue marks the return routed through treatment for reuse. The cooling tower removes heat before the water recirculates.
